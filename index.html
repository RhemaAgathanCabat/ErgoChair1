Frequently Asked Questions

💡 Do you deliver outside of Urdaneta City?

Yes! We provide safe door-to-door transit to homes and offices throughout different municipalities across Pangasinan. Shipping fees may vary depending on your specific location.

🛠️ Is the on-site chair assembly completely free?

Yes, our professional assembly service is completely free with your purchase. Our expert team will carefully build and test all complex hydraulic and tension mechanisms directly at your office or home so it is ready to use immediately.

📐 Can I test or customize the chair settings before paying?

Absolutely. Upon delivery, our technician will guide you through all custom settings—including the lumbar support curve, armrest rotations, and tilt tension lock—ensuring the chair is perfectly tailored to your posture size before final handoff.

🛡️ Do your ergonomic chairs come with a warranty?

Yes, all of our executive ergonomic models come with a standard warranty covering structural defects, hydraulic gas lift cylinders, and mechanical bases to ensure long-term spinal investment protection.
